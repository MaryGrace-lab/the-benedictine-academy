About our School

The Benedictine Academy is a premier Catholic educational institution founded on the 18th of September, 2017, dedicated to providing a holistic education rooted in the teachings of Jesus Christ. Our mission is to nurture the spiritual, intellectual, and personal growth of each student, preparing them to be compassionate leaders and responsible citizens. Located in the heart of the city, our school features state-of-the-art facilities, including modern classrooms, science and computer labs, a library, and sports facilities.

Our Mission

To provide a Christ-centered learning environment where every student is nurtured
spiritually, academically, and personally. We are dedicated to forming young hearts
and minds in the Catholic faith, preparing them to be compassionate leaders and
service-oriented citizens who contribute to the Kingdom of God on Earth.

Our Vision

To be a beacon of Catholic education, recognized for academic excellence and the
formation of virtuous individuals. We envision a community where faith is a living
force, inspiring students to pursue knowledge, practice charity, and live a life
rooted in Christ's teachings.

Our Core Values

  • Faith: Living out our beliefs through prayer, reverence, and spiritual growth.
  • Service: Following Christ's example by serving others with humility and love.
  • Integrity: Upholding honesty and moral principles in all actions.
  • Community: Fostering a supportive and welcoming family bound by shared faith and respect.
  • Excellence: Striving for academic and personal greatness as a way to honor God.
