Online Registration Procedure

Click on the "Click Here to Apply Now" to fill an online admission form.

  1. 1. Enter the student's infomation such as Full Name, DOB, etc.
    2. Enter the parent/guardian information such as Full name, a valid email address, etc.
    3. Review all the information you have entered to ensure accuracy and check the declaration box.
    4. Click on the "Submit Application" button to complete your registration.
    5. After completing the registration form online, make sure to print the completed form out for your records and bring to the school's admission office at 37, Old Mechanic Road, Off Siluko Road Egor, Benin City, Edo State.
    Submit the following documents at the school's admission office:
      -2 passport photographs of the applicant
      -Photocopy of applicant’s last class result (where applicable)
      -Photocopy of applicant’s Birth Certificate/ immunization record
    N/B: We recommend that you should use one email address for each student registration.

Ensure to complete your application form with the required information before heading to the office of the administrator.
