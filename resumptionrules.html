School Resumption Guidelines

We are excited to welcome back our students for the new academic year! Classes will resume on September 8, 2025. Please ensure that all school supplies are ready and uniforms are in order. Below are the resumption guidelines:

  • School gate opens for daily resumption between 7am – 8am and locks at 8am. (The school gate will not be opened for any child who comes later than the stated time above.)
  • Pupils and students must be neatly dressed in school uniform with plain white socks and black full covered shoes Mondays to Thursdays.
  • Pupils and students must be dressed in their appropriate sport wears every Friday with plain white sneakers/trainers only. Colored sneakers are not allowed.
  • Notebooks and textbooks are to be bought from school.
